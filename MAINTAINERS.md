# Maintainers

Shōkai is stewarded by Kigenka for now. [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md) explains how decisions are made and the path to a neutral home.

## Editor

| Role | Acting for | Areas |
|---|---|---|
| The editor | Kigenka | Everything: the specification, the extensions, the conformance suite, the SDK and the reference implementations |

## Maintainers

None yet besides the editor. Regular contributors can become maintainers. GOVERNANCE.md says what a maintainer may merge. It also says when the open call for maintainers and a charter start: two or more independent implementations that interoperate, outside maintainers, or adopters.

## Contact

- Questions and ideas: GitHub Discussions.
- Problems and concrete changes: GitHub issues (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Security: privately, as [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) explains; security@kigenka.com.
- Conduct: as the [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) explains.
