# Trademark policy

> "Shōkai" and "Kigenka" are names, and the arch-and-keystone mark is a logo, used for this project under the Kigenka steward brand (held by Yu Sung Yeh until Kigenka is formed as a legal entity). **None of them is a registered trademark**, and nothing here claims a registration. Write "Shōkai", never "Shōkai®". Filings are planned once there is adoption. The Shōkai protocol name and the Kigenka commercial brand are separate: Shōkai names the protocol; Kigenka names the steward and its commercial services.

The Apache License 2.0 (§6) and CC BY 4.0 (§2(b)(2)) don't grant any rights in names or logos. This page says how you may use them, so that people can trust that something called Shōkai is Shōkai. It covers three separate things: **descriptive compatibility** (truthful statements about what you built, below; no permission needed), **official project marks** (the names used as branding, and the logo), and the **conformance listing** (below).

## Fine without asking

- **Describing what you built, accurately:** "implements Shōkai", "a Shōkai door", "compatible with Shōkai v0.5", "passes the Shōkai core conformance suite (v0.5.0)". Name the version and, where it matters, the conformance level or extensions.
- **Talking about the project:** articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons and reviews, including critical ones.
- **Linking** to the specification or this repository with the plain word "Shōkai".
- **Japanese:** 紹介 is an everyday word meaning "introduction". Nothing here limits its ordinary use; this policy is only about using it as the name of this protocol.

## Not allowed

- **Implying endorsement, partnership or approval** by Kigenka or the Shōkai project, or that Kigenka has reviewed your product, unless it has, in writing.
- **"Official" or "approved" claims, or a badge that suggests either.** Shōkai has no badge, mark or approval program. Passing the conformance runner is something you may state factually (with the version), not an endorsement.
- **Shipping a modified specification or fork under the Shōkai name.** Forks are welcome under the licences, but **a fork must use a different name**. You may say it is "based on Shōkai v0.5.0" or "derived from the Shōkai specification".
- **Using the logo** on your product, packaging, app icon or marketing without written permission. You may show it, unchanged, when writing about the project.
- **Names that could be confused** with Shōkai or Kigenka for agent, identity or introduction products or services (for example a product, domain or package called "Shokai Pro" or "Kigenka Agents").
- **Package names** that suggest an official release, such as anything under the `@kigenka` npm scope or a top-level `shokai` package that isn't published by the project.

## The conformance listing (planned)

Kigenka may offer, for a fee, to run the open conformance suite on a product and publish the result: **a factual test report and an entry in a public listing** (product, version, suite, date, result). It comes with **no badge, logo or mark**, and a listed product may not call itself officially approved. Anyone may run the same suite and state their own results. Any badge or approval program would need separate, published rules first.

## Other projects' marks

Shōkai's documents name other technologies (for example the Model Context Protocol, A2A, OAuth, WebAuthn and passkeys) only to describe compatibility. Those names belong to their owners. Model Context Protocol and MCP are trademarks of LF Projects, LLC. Naming a technology implies no affiliation or endorsement.

## Questions and permission

Open an issue titled "Trademark question", or ask privately at security@kigenka.com. That inbox handles security, Code of Conduct and trademark reports for now; put "Trademark question" in the subject line.

If Shōkai moves to neutral stewardship (see "Path to a neutral home" in [GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/Kigenka/shokai/blob/v0.5.0/GOVERNANCE.md)), what may move is **only the Shōkai protocol name (word mark) and a designated Shōkai protocol logo**, with their goodwill and under written transfer terms; that foundation's trademark policy then replaces this one for them. **The Kigenka name, its logo and its commercial services are expressly reserved**: they stay with their owner unless separately agreed in writing. Which logo is designated as the protocol logo will be recorded before any transfer.
