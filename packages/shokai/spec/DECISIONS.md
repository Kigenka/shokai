# Design decisions

The `[D…]` tags in [SPEC.md](./SPEC.md) and the extensions point here. Each entry says what was decided, why, and where it lives. Decisions are made by the editor after review; changes to them start as a numbered proposal ([CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/Kigenka/shokai/blob/v0.3.0/CONTRIBUTING.md)). Dates are Pacific Time.

| ID | Decision | Date | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Introducer penalties are graded, local and limited.** Minor: none. Serious: `reduced` for 90 days. Severe: `suspended` for 180 days, for the relied-on vouch's purposes at that gatekeeper only. A revocation within 7 days halves the duration; a signed `IntroducerReview` restores standing early. | 2026-10-24 | §4, §9.3 |
| D2 | **Requests beyond the mandate, in a fixed order:** narrow to the in-mandate actions; lower the limit to the mandate's; if nothing in-mandate is left, `considering` when policy allows and the visitor carries a full-weight vouch; otherwise `deny` / `not_now` / `MANDATE_SCOPE_EXCEEDED`. | 2026-10-24 | §6.7 |
| D3 | **Our own card format and signing.** A Shōkai card with a published W3C VC 2.0 mapping; JCS (RFC 8785) for canonical JSON; owners sign with passkeys (WebAuthn, ES256), representatives with Ed25519; reputation and relationship memory stay local; the helper direction is deferred. | 2026-10-24 | §2, W3C VC mapping extension |
| D4 | **Chatham House quoting:** paraphrase by default with identifying hints stripped; verbatim only with the speaker's consent. | 2026-10-24 | Gatherings extension |
| D5 | **Pressing after a privacy decline:** thresholds in the house rules (light flags first, a minor notice at the third press), counted per asker, place and topic for 30 days. | 2026-10-24 | §9.3, Gatherings extension |
| D6 | **Linking a return visit:** a one-time visit receipt from the lobby, echoed inside a later signed request; a bare reference links nothing. | 2026-10-24 | Visit receipts extension |
| D7 | **Owner consent:** one signed `OwnerConsent` (approve or decline, what was shown, a subject digest and an expiry); a passkey for anything binding, a chat reply for small things; silence is no; revocable. | 2026-10-24 | §8.1 |
| D8 | **House rules are accountability, not prevention:** signed, tagged items; leaks are misconduct; each owner consents to the house rules on entry. | 2026-10-24 | Gatherings extension |
| D9 | *Open.* Social proof (a signed post on a social network) as an optional credential a place may require. | — | not in the text |
| D10 | **Language scope:** an English-first pilot with a language-neutral core: registered codes instead of free text, strict amounts, reports rendered from machine fields in the owner's language, RFC 4647 locale matching, IANA time zones. | 2026-10-24 | §6.6, Meaning notes extension |
| D11 | **The standing allowance and the duty of loyalty:** an owner signs an allowance once; inside it the representative acts without prompting the owner, outside it the request is taken home and silence is no; no spending allowance means no spending; peer meetings are one shared encounter seeded by a both-owner invitation; a representative owes loyalty to its principal. | 2026-10-24 | §1, §5.2, §7.2, Peer meetings extension |
| D12 | **Allowance status lifetime:** a signed `AllowanceStatus` is valid for at most one hour. | 2026-10-25 | §5.2, Revocation status extension |
| D13 | *Reserved* for a proposal on readiness and growth. | — | — |
| D14 | **Red-team round 2:** "accept any introducer" gives no weight for money or account creation; a `per_operation` seat keeps a session-wide cap; at the encounter cap a place refuses newcomers and never evicts guests. | 2026-10-26 | §3.4, §4, §7.2 |
| D15 | **The inbound door and owner-level conduct:** a person's representative receives brands at its own door; a representative's conduct is its owner's conduct; consequences stay local; taste is never misconduct; optional kind reason categories. | 2026-10-26 | §9.3, §10 |
| D16 | **Approaches that think of the other person:** `manipulative_pressure` (false urgency, scarcity, familiarity, claimed consent); rewordings of one pitch are one campaign; minor signals add up. | 2026-10-26 | §9.3, §10 |
| D17 | **Account creation by place policy:** each place decides whether sign-up needs an introduction or its own invitation; places offering accounts or money should require one; an introduction is always a deliberate act. | 2026-10-26 | §4 |
| D18 | **Core plus extensions:** a core every implementation needs and eleven optional extensions, each with its own document and conformance level, versioned with the core and never weakening it. | 2026-10-26 | Conventions, extensions/ |
| D19 | **The v0.3 release choices:** the name Shōkai and wire tag `shokai/0.3`; sections in story order; fictional actors in every example while real technologies keep their names; declared data asks as a SHOULD. | 2026-10-26 | the whole text |
| D20 | **Website, agent front, door:** the ordinary website is outside Shōkai; the agent front (listing and lobby) is open to look at anonymously; anything personal happens at the door; limits apply from the door onward. | 2026-10-26 | §3, §3.1, §12 |
| D21 | **Security contact:** GitHub private vulnerability reporting first, security@kigenka.com as the email alternative. | 2026-10-26 | SECURITY.md |
| D22 | **Per-place owner identity:** owner identities are per place by default and unlinkable by key: one passkey credential per place, so every owner signature stays a user-verified assertion. The owner chooses when to link two identities, for one place, with a link both passkeys sign. Linking adds history and never clears it. Content, timing and network can still link; the text says so. | 2026-10-27 | §2.8, §12.3 |
| D23 | **One tap for several items:** optional; one passkey assertion over 2 to 16 items, every item shown to the owner first, each item verifiable on its own. | 2026-10-27 | Appendix A.1 |
| D24 | **Signing pages:** owners sign on a passkey signing page with a fixed profile (exact origin, no cross-origin ceremony). Anyone can run one; Kigenka runs a free hosted page while the protocol is in draft, outside the specification and with no uptime promise. | 2026-10-27 | Appendix A.1, [SIGNING.md](./SIGNING.md) |
| D25 | **Representative continuity and history** (partly decided; [P20](https://github.com/Kigenka/shokai/blob/v0.3.0/proposals/P20-representative-continuity.md)). A representative belongs to its owner, not the agent vendor; a company's representative belongs to the company. A Shōkai-compatible agent MUST let its owner export the full Shōkai history (cards, receipts, introductions, successions and handovers, lineage) in `shokai-history/1`; memory export is SHOULD, envelope only. Owner-signed succession is add-only; an optional handover ceremony inspired by *hikitsugi* has the retiring representative introduce its successor at places the owner approved. A signed `never` receipt is SHOULD; receipts don't expire (places MAY weigh recency); limits of 14 days overlap, 10 minutes presentation, 30 days handover, 30 days `vouch_honoured`. Continuity is planned as an optional extension in v0.3; place receipts wait for v0.4 after a two-place field test. *Open:* field-level selective disclosure, `seq` leakage, shared recovery with P18, registry entries. | 2026-10-27 | P20 (not in the text yet) |
| D26 | **rc4 limits accepted as documented:** refuse-on-loss can't catch a state loss nobody noticed (§3.6, THREAT-MODEL); a valid owner link carries `never` across the linked identities (§2.8); the SDK's default door keeps state in memory only and refuses mandates older than the door. | 2026-10-27 | §2.8, §3.6, the SDK |

rc4 also adds card revocation (§2.6), durable state with refuse-on-loss (§3.6), and the act check order and operation counting (§7.2). These fill gaps found in field tests and review rather than choosing between options, so they carry check numbers (C191–C194), not decision ids.
