About this signing page

This signing page is an early, test-stage convenience. Shōkai is still a draft, and Kigenka runs this page while it's being tested.

Your passkey stays on your phone or computer. Kigenka never sees it and can't sign for you.

A passkey works only on the web address it was made on, here sign.kigenka.com. We plan to keep this address. If it ever has to change, we'll tell you at least 90 days in advance. A way to sign a move to a new key with the passkey you have now, so the places that know your representative can keep recognizing it, is planned (proposal P24, for v0.4) but doesn't exist yet; we won't move the address before it does.

Everything you've already signed stays valid either way.

You don't have to use this page. Anyone can sign with their own signing page or their own key; see "Signing on your own" below.

What if the signing page's address changes?

sign.kigenka.com is an early, test-stage convenience run by Kigenka while Shōkai is a draft. A passkey works only on the web address it was made on, so we intend to keep this address. If it ever has to move, owners will be told at least 90 days in advance, on this page, on the Shōkai site and in the repository, and given a way to move to a new key, signed with their current one, before the old address stops signing. That way is planned (proposal P24, for v0.4) and doesn't exist yet; the address won't move before it does. Everything already signed stays valid.

What we never promise

An uptime, a date, or that a move needs nothing from you.

Signing on your own

Run the reference signing page on your own domain and register your passkey there, or, for an organization, sign with a key published at your own domain (did:web). Places check every owner signature the same way, whatever page made it; this page gets no special treatment.
