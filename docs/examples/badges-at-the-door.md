# Badges at the door

**Proposal, not built in v0.5.0.** Shōkai v0.5.0 does not read Open Badges or other achievement credentials.

What's proposed: a door may ask for a badge as a signed proof, presented alongside the Shōkai visitor card. The visitor card is not upgraded. It still says who the person is and who stands behind the representative. Each door decides what a badge is worth. The owner chooses what to show, so a door does not see a whole record. There is no global score.

The closest piece already in the protocol is an optional vouch ([SPEC §4](../../SPEC.md)), which is not an achievement credential. [Open Badges 3.0](https://standards.1edtech.org/open-badges/specifications/standards/v3p0) is a 1EdTech standard, built on the [W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model-2.0/).

The worked example, including Ines's "Data Ethics, gold" badge at a lab or a scholarship desk, is in [An online university](./online-university.md#proposal-badges-that-open-doors). The same proposal is listed under [Future directions](../future-directions.md).
