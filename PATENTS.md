## Why there is a separate commitment

The specification is licensed under the Apache License 2.0. Its §3 gives a patent licence from each contributor for claims that their contribution, alone or combined with the work, necessarily infringes. That clearly covers copying and changing the specification text and the code in this repository. Whether it also covers an **independent implementation** written from the specification alone is less clear, because that implementation isn't "the Work".

Standards bodies close that gap with a separate royalty-free commitment that covers implementations. For Shōkai we plan to use the **[Open Web Foundation Final Specification Agreement 1.0 (OWFa 1.0)](https://www.openwebfoundation.org/the-agreements/the-owf-1-0-agreements-granted-claims/owfa-1-0)**, a short, widely used agreement that a single steward can make for its own specification.

## What the copyright holder intends to commit to

For **each final (non-release-candidate) version** of the Shōkai specification published by the project, starting with v0.3.0, Yu Sung Yeh intends to make the OWFa 1.0 commitment for that version. In summary (the agreement's own text governs, not this summary):

- **Copyright:** a perpetual, worldwide, no-charge, royalty-free licence to reproduce, adapt, distribute and implement the specification, with attribution naming the specification and its version.
- **Patents:** an irrevocable promise not to assert the signer's "Granted Claims" (claims necessarily infringed by implementing the parts of the specification it describes in detail) against anyone making, using, selling or distributing an implementation, to the extent it implements the specification and implements all of its required portions. It is backed by a commitment to license those claims royalty-free on reasonable and non-discriminatory terms.
- **Defensive termination:** the promise ends for anyone who sues the signer claiming that implementing the specification infringes their own patents.
- **No other rights:** no trademark rights (see [TRADEMARKS.md](./TRADEMARKS.md)), and no assurance that third parties hold no relevant patents.

The commitment covers the **core specification and the extensions** of the version named. Release candidates (for example v0.3.0-rc1) are drafts for review and aren't covered until the final version is published.

Contributors aren't asked to sign the OWFa; a future steering group or foundation may adopt a fuller patent policy for participants (see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md)).
